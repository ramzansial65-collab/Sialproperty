Before you make a property decision

01 · OwnershipVerify title documents, ownership history and the seller's identity.
02 · ApprovalsCheck the relevant authority's NOC, approved layout and permitted use.
03 · Site VisitVisit the actual location and confirm boundaries, access and nearby facilities.
04 · DuesConfirm taxes, utility bills, society charges and any outstanding liabilities.
05 · TransferUnderstand transfer fees, taxes, documentation and the official payment process.
06 · Legal ReviewAsk a qualified property lawyer to review documents and agreements where needed.
