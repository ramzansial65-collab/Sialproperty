HOW TO USE

Plan with clear assumptions

Choose Buy, Rent or Investment and enter your own figures. Results update as you change the inputs.

01Buy: estimates the remaining property price divided by the selected months. It assumes zero interest.
02Rent: estimates total rent using the annual increase you enter.
03Investment: estimates gross rental yield and annual net income from your entered rent and expenses.
These are planning estimates, not bank offers, valuations, tax advice or guaranteed investment returns. Actual charges, taxes, vacancy, maintenance and legal costs may change the result.
