BEFORE INVESTING

Questions worth checking

01Is the title clear, and does the seller have authority to transfer the property?
02Are the layout, approvals and land use confirmed with the relevant authority?
03What are the realistic rent, vacancy periods and maintenance costs?
04Have taxes, transfer fees, utility dues and society charges been checked?
05Can you afford the investment if rent is delayed or the property remains vacant?
Property prices and rental income can fall as well as rise. Verify documents with the relevant authority and consult qualified legal, tax and financial professionals where appropriate.
