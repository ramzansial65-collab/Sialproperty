Property Buyer Checklist

  • Original ownership/title documents checked
  • Seller identity and authority verified
  • Relevant NOC / approved layout confirmed with authority
  • Property physically visited and boundaries checked
  • Outstanding taxes, utility bills and society dues checked
  • Transfer fees, taxes and payment schedule understood
  • Agreement reviewed by a qualified property lawyer
  • Payments made through traceable, documented channels

This is a general checklist, not legal advice. Requirements differ by location and property type.
